    Experience Points 

    Earn XP by completing courses and solving problems. The more difficult the Go problem, the higher its rank, the more XP you can earn.

    Coins 

    Coins are awarded for solving problems on your first attempt and completing courses. Save up your coins for something special from the Go Magic store.

    Please note: Solving problems simpler than your account level leads to reduced XP and Coin rewards.

    Go Magic Leaders and Their Rewards

    On the leaders page you will find the top ranking students of our platform: for one day, week and month. At the end of each one of these periods the most hard-working students get rewarded accordingly.

    For a day: 1–5 coins. For a week: 5–30 coins. For a month: 5–100 coins.

    The coins you earn also affect the ranking. Every coin adds 100 extra points to the overall number of XP.
